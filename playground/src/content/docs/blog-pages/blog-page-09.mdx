## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add blog/blog-page-09
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/two-col-with-sidebar](/docs/header-navigation-sections/two-col-with-sidebar), [blog/simple-06](/docs/blog-sections/simple-06), [blog/split-layout-02](/docs/blog-sections/split-layout-02), [cta/simple-centered](/docs/cta-sections/simple-centered), [footers/large-05](/docs/footers-sections/large-05). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (blog/simple-06) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
