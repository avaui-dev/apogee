## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add blog/blog-page-05
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [banner/slim-brand-full-width](/docs/banner-sections/slim-brand-full-width), [header-navigation/dropdown-featured-posts](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-featured-posts), [blog/featured-post-03](/docs/blog-sections/featured-post-03), [blog/carousel-layout-02](/docs/blog-sections/carousel-layout-02), [cta/split-image-quote-02](/docs/cta-sections/split-image-quote-02), [footers/large-02](/docs/footers-sections/large-02). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (blog/featured-post-03) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
