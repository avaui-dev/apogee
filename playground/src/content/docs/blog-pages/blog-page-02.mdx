## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add blog/blog-page-02
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-simple-with-footer](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple-with-footer), [blog/alt-layout-02](/docs/blog-sections/alt-layout-02), [cta/iphone-mockup-02](/docs/cta-sections/iphone-mockup-02), [footers/large-09](/docs/footers-sections/large-09). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (blog/alt-layout-02) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
