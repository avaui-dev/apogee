## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add footers/large-06
```

This copies the block to `src/components/apogee/blocks/footers/` with everything it uses: Badge, Button, FeaturedIcon, Heading, Input, Link, Rating, Section. Replace the sample data at the top of the file with yours, or use **Copy code** above to paste it instead.

## Accessibility

- The heading is an `h2` by default, so the block slots into a page under its `h1`; item titles are one level down.
- The email field is named "Email" (the placeholder is not a label); the form submits without JS.
- The icon links are named (Email, Blog feed, Community…); the icons themselves are hidden.
- The stars are read as one rating ("Rated 5 out of 5").
- The block is the page's `<footer>` landmark; each link column is a `<nav>` named after its heading.
