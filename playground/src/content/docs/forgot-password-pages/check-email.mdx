## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add forgot-password/check-email
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/forgot-password/` with everything it uses: AuthShell, Button, FeaturedIcon, Link. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the "Back to log in" arrow flips under right-to-left text.
- "Open email app" is a `mailto:` link; on a phone it opens the default mail app.
- "Click to resend" is a link: point it at your resend endpoint.
