## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add forgot-password/sidebar
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/forgot-password/` with everything it uses: AuthShell, Button, FeaturedIcon, Input, ProgressIndicator, Steps. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the "Back to log in" arrow flips under right-to-left text.
- Below `lg` the side column hides; the dots under the form still say "step 1 of 4" to screen readers.
