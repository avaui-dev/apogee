Each example is a block: a `<footer>` with link columns or a row of links,
and some mix of a newsletter signup, a call to action, app buttons, a rating
and social links over a copyright line.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add footers/large-01
```

Social links use generic icons (email, feed, community, website); put your
own network marks in their place.

## Copy is props

Every block in this family takes the same content props (`FooterContent` in
`sections/_shared/footers.ts`), with its sample copy as the defaults. A block
ignores the props it has no place for, so you can swap one block for another and
keep the copy. The [FooterSection](/docs/sections/footer-section) section takes the same names for the props they share.

- `brand`: The name in the logo or wordmark.
- `tagline`: A short line about the company. Wrap words in *asterisks* to accent them where the block styles an accent.
- `heading`: A call to action above or beside the links. Wrap words in *asterisks* to accent them.
- `description`: The line under the heading.
- `primaryAction`: The solid button.
- `secondaryAction`: The outline button.
- `newsletterHeading`: The label or heading over the email signup.
- `newsletterDescription`: The line under the newsletter heading.
- `submitLabel`: The signup button.
- `columns`: Link groups. Blocks with one flat row of links show every column's links in that row.
- `navLabel`: Name of the flat link row's nav.
- `copyright`: The copyright line.
- `legal`: Small links beside the copyright (terms, privacy).
- `social`: Icon links.

- Pass `null` to hide an optional part, such as `description={null}`.
- Some blocks add props of their own; the top of each block's file lists them.
