Each example is a block: a `<footer>` with link columns or a row of links,
and some mix of a newsletter signup, a call to action, app buttons, a rating
and social links over a copyright line.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add footers/large-01
```

Social links use generic icons (email, feed, community, website); put your
own network marks in their place.
