## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add sign-up/sidebar-progress
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/sign-up/` with everything it uses: AuthShell, Button, FeaturedIcon, Input, PasswordChecklist, ProgressIndicator, Steps. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the fields carry visible labels and `autocomplete` hints (`new-password` for new passwords, so password managers offer to generate one).
- Below `lg` the side column hides; the dots under the form still say "step 2 of 4" to screen readers.
