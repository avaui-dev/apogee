## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add sign-up/split-image-background
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/sign-up/` with everything it uses: AuthShell, AvatarGroup, Button, FeaturedIcon, Image, Input, Link, Rating. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the fields carry visible labels and `autocomplete` hints (`new-password` for new passwords, so password managers offer to generate one).
- The tint is the primary fill at 85%, with on-primary text, so the copy keeps its contrast over any photo.
- The SSO button is a link to your identity provider; swap its `href` for your own.
