## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add pricing/pricing-page-10
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/pricing/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-simple](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple), [pricing/simple-icon-offset](/docs/pricing-sections/simple-icon-offset), [faq/simple-02](/docs/faq-sections/simple-02), [cta/screen-mockup-04](/docs/cta-sections/screen-mockup-04), [footers/large-03](/docs/footers-sections/large-03). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (pricing/simple-icon-offset) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
