This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog-post/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/two-col-with-links](/docs/header-navigation-sections/two-col-with-links), [content/split-image-02](/docs/content-sections/split-image-02), [blog/carousel-layout-02](/docs/blog-sections/carousel-layout-02), [footers/large-09](/docs/footers-sections/large-09). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (content/split-image-02) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
