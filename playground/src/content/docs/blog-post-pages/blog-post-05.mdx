## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add blog-post/blog-post-05
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog-post/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-featured-posts](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-featured-posts), [content/split-image-01](/docs/content-sections/split-image-01), [blog/carousel-layout-01](/docs/blog-sections/carousel-layout-01), [cta/card-horizontal](/docs/cta-sections/card-horizontal), [footers/large-16](/docs/footers-sections/large-16). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (content/split-image-01) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
