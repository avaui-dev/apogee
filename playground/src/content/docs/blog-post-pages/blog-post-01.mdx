## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add blog-post/blog-post-01
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/blog-post/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-feature-card](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-feature-card), [content/alternative-02](/docs/content-sections/alternative-02), [blog/simple-left-aligned-01](/docs/blog-sections/simple-left-aligned-01), [cta/abstract-images](/docs/cta-sections/abstract-images), [newsletter/card-horizontal](/docs/newsletter-sections/card-horizontal), [footers/large-01](/docs/footers-sections/large-01). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (content/alternative-02) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
