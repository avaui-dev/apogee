## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add video-player
```

Uses `@lucide/astro` (`Play`).

## Usage

```astro
---
import VideoPlayer from '@/components/apogee/VideoPlayer/VideoPlayer.astro'
---
<VideoPlayer src="/media/tour.mp4" poster="/media/tour.jpg" captions="/media/tour.vtt" title="A tour of the reports page" />
```

## API

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `src` | `string` | — | The video file (MP4 or WebM). |
| `title` | `string` | — | What the video shows; names the play button ("Play video: …"). |
| `poster` | `string` | — | The image shown before it plays. |
| `captions` | `string` | — | A WebVTT captions file, on by default. |
| `captionsLang` | `string` | `'en'` | Language of the captions. |
| `ratio` | `'16/9' \| '16/10' \| '4/3' \| '1/1'` | `'16/9'` | Reserves the space before the video loads. |
| `radius` | `'none' \| 'small' \| 'medium' \| 'large' \| 'full'` | `'large'` | Corner rounding. `full` is the largest rounding a video takes without cropping the picture. |
| `class` | `string` | — | Size it here (`max-w-3xl`, `w-full`). |

## States

- **Before play** — the poster and the play button; the browser's controls are hidden.
- **Playing** — the button is gone and the browser's controls take over: pause, seek, volume, captions, full screen.

Feature sections with a video: [Features sections](/docs/features-sections).

## Accessibility

- The play button is named "Play video: \{title\}"; after it, focus moves to the video, where the browser's controls take the keyboard.
- `preload="none"`: nothing downloads until someone presses play.
- Give the video captions (`captions`) when it has speech or text that matters.
- The play button's hover growth respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Tokens

| Token | Used for |
|---|---|
| `--background-inverted` | Behind the video while it loads |
| `--background-page` | Play button disc |
| `--shadow-raised` | Lift |
| `--duration-fast` / `ease-standard` | Play button hover |

## Do / Don't

- **Do** set a `poster`; without one the frame is a dark box until it plays.
- **Don't** autoplay with sound.

## Content

- Write `title` for what happens in the video ("A tour of the reports page"), not "Video".
