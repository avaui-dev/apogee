
This copies the block to `src/components/apogee/blocks/header/` with everything it uses: Button, Input, Link, Section, SectionHeader. Replace the sample data at the top of the file with yours, or use **Copy code** above to paste it instead.

## Accessibility

- The heading is the page's `h1`; the items below are one level down.
- The email field is named "Email" (the placeholder is not a label); the form submits without JS.
