## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add legal/legal-page-03
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/legal/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [banner/slim-brand-full-width](/docs/banner-sections/slim-brand-full-width), [header-navigation/dropdown-simple-two-columns](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple-two-columns), [header/space-between-search](/docs/header-sections/space-between-search), [content/rich-text-02](/docs/content-sections/rich-text-02), [cta/simple-logos-02](/docs/cta-sections/simple-logos-02), [footers/large-06](/docs/footers-sections/large-06). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (header/space-between-search) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
