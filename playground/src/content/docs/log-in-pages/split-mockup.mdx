## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add log-in/split-mockup
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/log-in/` with everything it uses: AuthShell (layout split), Card, Stat, Table, Badge. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the form fields carry visible labels and `autocomplete` hints, so password managers fill them.
- The product screen is decoration: it is `aria-hidden` and `inert`, so screen readers and the Tab key skip it.
- The SSO button is a link to your identity provider; swap its `href` for your own.
