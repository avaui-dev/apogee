## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add log-in/simple
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/log-in/` with everything it uses: AuthShell, Tabs, Input, Checkbox, Button, Link, FeaturedIcon. Render it from one of your routes, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`; the form fields carry visible labels and `autocomplete` hints, so password managers fill them.
- The "Sign up / Log in" switch is a Tabs list whose Sign up tab is a link, so it navigates instead of showing a panel.
- The SSO button is a link to your identity provider; swap its `href` for your own.
