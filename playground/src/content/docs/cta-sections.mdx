Each example is a block: a heading, a line and one or two actions, with
logos, a photo, a sliding testimonial, the product in a
[DeviceFrame](/docs/device-frame) (a phone or a browser window), an image
collage or a card around it.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add cta/simple-centered
```

The phone and browser blocks show screenshots from `/mockups/` (the
Apogee dashboard); put your own product's screenshots there, a light and a
dark one.

## Copy is props

Every block in this family takes the same content props (`CTAContent` in
`sections/_shared/cta.ts`), with its sample copy as the defaults. A block
ignores the props it has no place for, so you can swap one block for another and
keep the copy. The [CTA](/docs/sections/cta) section takes the same names for the props they share.

- `eyebrow`: The small label above the heading.
- `heading`: The heading.
- `description`: The line under the heading.
- `primaryAction`: The solid button (the first of two equal buttons).
- `secondaryAction`: The outline button.
- `media`: The photo or screenshot beside or under the copy.

- Pass `null` to hide an optional part, such as `description={null}`.
- Some blocks add props of their own; the top of each block's file lists them.
