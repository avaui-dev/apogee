
This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/faq/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-simple](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple), [header/centered-search](/docs/header-sections/centered-search), [faq/accordion-02](/docs/faq-sections/accordion-02), [content/section-split-image-02](/docs/content-sections/section-split-image-02), [cta/split-image-03](/docs/cta-sections/split-image-03), [footers/large-09](/docs/footers-sections/large-09). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (header/centered-search) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
