## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add contact/contact-page-10
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/contact/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [contact/simple-form-05](/docs/contact-sections/simple-form-05). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (contact/simple-form-05) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
