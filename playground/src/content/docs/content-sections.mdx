The first twelve are whole article pages: a header (centred, split or on the
brand colour) with a photo, the article in [Prose](/docs/prose), and a footer
with the author, topics and [ShareLinks](/docs/share-links), some with a
[TableOfContents](/docs/table-of-contents) and a newsletter card beside the
body. The rest are content sections: text in columns, text beside an image,
a quote, and long-form policy text.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add content/large-image-01
```

Put your article in place of the sample body; render Markdown into `Prose`
with `<Prose><Content /></Prose>`.
