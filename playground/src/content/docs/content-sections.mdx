The first twelve are whole article pages: a header (centred, split or on the
brand colour) with a photo, the article in [Prose](/docs/prose), and a footer
with the author, topics and [ShareLinks](/docs/share-links), some with a
[TableOfContents](/docs/table-of-contents) and a newsletter card beside the
body. The rest are content sections: text in columns, text beside an image,
a quote, and long-form policy text.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add content/large-image-01
```

Put your article in place of the sample body; render Markdown into `Prose`
with `<Prose><Content /></Prose>`.

## Copy is props

Every block in this family takes the same content props (`ContentContent` in
`sections/_shared/content.ts`), with its sample copy as the defaults. A block
ignores the props it has no place for, so you can swap one block for another and
keep the copy. The [Content](/docs/sections/content) section takes the same names for the props they share.

- `eyebrow`: The small label or category above the heading.
- `heading`: The heading.
- `description`: The line under the heading.
- `label`: Landmark name when the block shows no heading.
- `primaryAction`: The solid button (the first of two equal buttons).
- `secondaryAction`: The outline button.
- `media`: The main photo beside or under the heading.
- `author`: Who wrote it (or who is quoted).
- `date`: ISO date, e.g. `2026-01-20`.
- `readingTime`: e.g. "8 min read".
- `topics`: Topic badges under the article.

- Pass `null` to hide an optional part, such as `description={null}`.
- Some blocks add props of their own; the top of each block's file lists them.
