## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add not-found/split-search
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/not-found/` with everything it uses: Button, FeaturedIcon, Image, Input, NotFound. Render it from `src/pages/404.astro`, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`.
- The search is a `role="search"` form that sends `q` to `/search` with GET; point `action` at your own search page.
- The photo has empty alt text: it is decoration.
