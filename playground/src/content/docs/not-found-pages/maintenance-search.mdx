
This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/not-found/` with everything it uses: Button, Input, NotFound. Render it from `src/pages/404.astro`, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page heading is the `h1`.
- The search is a `role="search"` form that sends `q` to `/search` with GET; point `action` at your own search page.
- The large 503 is decoration and hides below `lg`.
- For real downtime, serve it with status 503 and a `Retry-After` header.
