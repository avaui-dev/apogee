```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add landing/landing-page-11
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/landing/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-feature-card](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-feature-card), [hero/card-mockup-09](/docs/hero-sections/card-mockup-09), [features/simple-icons-02](/docs/features-sections/simple-icons-02), [pricing/simple-cards-03](/docs/pricing-sections/simple-cards-03), [testimonials/glassmorphic-cards-01](/docs/testimonials-sections/glassmorphic-cards-01), [social-proof/card](/docs/social-proof-sections/card), [cta/screen-mockup-01](/docs/cta-sections/screen-mockup-01), [footers/large-11](/docs/footers-sections/large-11). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (hero/card-mockup-09) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
