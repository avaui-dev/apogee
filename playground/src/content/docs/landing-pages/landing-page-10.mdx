## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add landing/landing-page-10
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/landing/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-simple](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple), [hero/card-mockup-02](/docs/hero-sections/card-mockup-02), [social-proof/full-width-masked](/docs/social-proof-sections/full-width-masked), [testimonials/case-study-cards](/docs/testimonials-sections/case-study-cards), [features/tabs-mockup-02](/docs/features-sections/tabs-mockup-02), [social-proof/press-mentions](/docs/social-proof-sections/press-mentions), [blog/simple-left-aligned-01](/docs/blog-sections/simple-left-aligned-01), [cta/simple-logos-02](/docs/cta-sections/simple-logos-02), [newsletter/simple-left](/docs/newsletter-sections/simple-left), [footers/large-10](/docs/footers-sections/large-10). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (hero/card-mockup-02) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
