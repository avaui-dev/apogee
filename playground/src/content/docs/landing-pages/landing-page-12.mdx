## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add landing/landing-page-12
```

This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/landing/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/dropdown-simple-with-footer](/docs/header-navigation-sections/dropdown-simple-with-footer), [hero/iphone-mockup-03](/docs/hero-sections/iphone-mockup-03), [features/tabs-mockup-09](/docs/features-sections/tabs-mockup-09), [cta/card-horizontal](/docs/cta-sections/card-horizontal), [faq/accordion-01](/docs/faq-sections/accordion-01), [cta/iphone-mockup-01](/docs/cta-sections/iphone-mockup-01), [newsletter/simple-centered](/docs/newsletter-sections/simple-centered), [footers/large-03](/docs/footers-sections/large-03). Render it from a page in `src/pages/` inside a layout that has no `<main>` of its own, or use **Copy code** above.

## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (hero/iphone-mockup-03) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
