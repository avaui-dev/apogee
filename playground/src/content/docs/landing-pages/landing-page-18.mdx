## Add


This copies the page to `src/components/apogee/pages/landing/` with the blocks it stacks and everything they use: [header-navigation/three-col-with-sidebar](/docs/header-navigation-sections/three-col-with-sidebar), [hero/split-image-06](/docs/hero-sections/split-image-06), [features/alternating-layout-03](/docs/features-sections/alternating-layout-03), [cta/card-horizontal](/docs/cta-sections/card-horizontal), [features/tabs-mockup-06](/docs/features-sections/tabs-mockup-06), [testimonials/card-split-image](/docs/testimonials-sections/card-split-image), [cta/screen-mockup-04](/docs/cta-sections/screen-mockup-04), [newsletter/simple-left](/docs/newsletter-sections/simple-left), [footers/large-10](/docs/footers-sections/large-10). ## Accessibility

- The page brings its own landmarks: the header and its navigation, one `<main>`, and the footer.
- The heading of the first section (hero/split-image-06) is the page's only `h1`; every other section heading is an `h2`, with item titles one level down.
- Each block keeps its own accessibility notes; follow the links above.
