## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add testimonials/simple-centered-03
```

This copies the block to `src/components/apogee/blocks/testimonials/` with everything it uses: Avatar, Section, Tabs. Every piece of copy is a prop, with the sample copy as its default. Pass yours, or `null` to hide a part; see [copy is props](/docs/testimonials-sections#copy-is-props).

## Accessibility

- The department tabs are a tab list with one panel each; without JS every panel shows, one after another.
