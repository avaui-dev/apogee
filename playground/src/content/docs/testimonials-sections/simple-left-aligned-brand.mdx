## Add

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add testimonials/simple-left-aligned-brand
```

This copies the block to `src/components/apogee/blocks/testimonials/` with everything it uses: Image, Rating, Section. Replace the sample data at the top of the file with yours, or use **Copy code** above to paste it instead.

## Accessibility

- The stars are read as one rating ("Rated 5 out of 5").
- Photos that only decorate have an empty `alt`; give yours one when it carries meaning.
