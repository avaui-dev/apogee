Each example is a complete page. The frame comes from the
[AuthShell](/docs/sections/auth-shell) section; the form inside is plain
composition, because it posts to your own backend. New passwords use
[PasswordChecklist](/docs/password-checklist), and multi-step flows show their
place with [Steps](/docs/steps) or [ProgressIndicator](/docs/progress-indicator).

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add sign-up/simple
```

`npx @ava-ui/apogee list` shows every page.
