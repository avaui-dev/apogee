Each example is a block built on [ContactForm](/docs/contact-form),
[ContactMethod](/docs/contact-method), [MapEmbed](/docs/map-embed) and
[WorldMap](/docs/world-map): a form beside a map or a photo, ways to reach
you as plain items or cards, store lists, a world map of offices, and
brand-colour versions.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add contact/simple-form
```

Each block holds its sample data at the top (`methods`, `stores`, `pins`);
replace it with yours, and point the form's `action` at your endpoint.

## Copy is props

Every block in this family takes the same content props (`ContactContent` in
`sections/_shared/contact.ts`), with its sample copy as the defaults. A block
ignores the props it has no place for, so you can swap one block for another and
keep the copy. The [Contact](/docs/sections/contact) section takes the same names for the props they share.

- `eyebrow`: The small label above the heading.
- `heading`: The heading.
- `description`: The line under the heading.
- `details`: Ways to reach you: email, phone, chat, office.
- `locations`: Offices or stores, shown as addresses, tabs or map pins.
- `media`: The photo beside or above the content.
- `action`: Form target.
- `method`
- `submitLabel`

- Pass `null` to hide an optional part, such as `description={null}`.
- Some blocks add props of their own; the top of each block's file lists them.
