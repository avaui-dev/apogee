A section is a whole band of a page: a hero, a pricing table, a settings
form. Each takes a `layout`, `background`, `spacing` and `headingLevel`, and
`apogee add <section>` copies it in with the components it uses.

```sh
npx @ava-ui/apogee add hero pricing    # copies the sections and their components
```
